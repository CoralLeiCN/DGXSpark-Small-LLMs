## Diagnosis

- Symptom: doubling the configured Mamba full-memory ratio from 4.59 to 10.0 increased admission from 33 to only 38.
- Root cause: ratio-based automatic sizing did not provision the 360 slots needed for 72 requests under the target's static-memory budget.
- Evidence: SGLang's startup line reports 190 Mamba slots and c38; 72 requests require `72 * 5 = 360` slots.

## Fix Or Change

Used SGLang's explicit `--max-mamba-cache-size 360` control in the next experiment instead of relying on automatic ratio sizing.

## Verification

The follow-up explicit-cache startup is recorded in `RUN-0005` and `RUN-0006`.

## Lesson

For an admission target tied to Mamba slots, set the required slot count directly and verify the log. A ratio is not a predictable capacity setting.

## Next Step

Set a 360-slot Mamba cache and adjust the static-memory budget if the KV allocator rejects it.
